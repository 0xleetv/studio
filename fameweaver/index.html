Craft Dimension: Character & Interiority Benchmark Scene 1
What FameWeaver Delivers

Technique: Let the Body Speak

Problem Identified:

Oliver's fear of thunder is stated, but never deepened. The reader sees the fear but doesn't feel it physically.

The Craft Principle:

Show internal emotion through physical sensation. Instead of telling the reader Oliver is scared, let his body reveal it — clenched fists, held breath, stomach drop.

Before: "Oliver was scared of the thunder."

After: "The first rumble hit and Oliver's fingers went white around the blanket's edge. His belly did that thing again — the cold, sinking thing — like swallowing a stone."

Pinpoint: Highlighted directly in Chapter 1, Paragraph 3, lines 2-4.
What Generic AI Says

Vague Chatbot Response

"Give Oliver a more subtle character arc and make his reactions more emotional."

The Breakdown: That's the entire suggestion. No explanation of what a character arc IS. No example of what "more subtle" looks like. No indication of where Oliver's arc falls flat.

Result: You leave with the same question you started with.

Craft Dimension: Dramatic Pacing Benchmark Scene 2
What FameWeaver Delivers

Technique: Narrative Compression

Problem Identified:

The middle section between Oliver hearing the thunder and discovering the beast has three descriptive paragraphs that kill velocity.

The Craft Principle:

Shorten the distance between tension and payoff. When stakes are high, cut atmospheric filler and accelerate physical action.

Before: 3 long paragraphs describing hallway shadows and rain against windows.

After: "The hallway stretched. Lightning flashed. Something growled from the basement."

Pinpoint: Flagged at Paragraphs 7-9 with an inline "Where to Improve" marker.
What Generic AI Says

Vague Chatbot Response

"Tighten pacing throughout the middle."

The Breakdown: Throughout... which chapter? Every paragraph? Where exactly is momentum lost? What does "tighten" mean in sentence construction? AI doesn't know.

Result: Zero guidance on what to actually cut or rewrite.

Craft Dimension: Climax & Discovery Benchmark Scene 3
What FameWeaver Delivers

Technique: The Delayed Reveal

Problem Identified:

The moment Oliver discovers the "beast" arrives abruptly. The reader is robbed of the emotional shift from dread to delightful relief.

The Craft Principle:

Let the reader figure it out one beat before the character does. Plant a sensory clue (a rhythmic rumble, a familiar scent) so the reader clicks first.

Before: "Oliver saw it was just the vacuum cleaner."

After: "The growling had a rhythm to it. Back and forth. Back and forth. And there was that warm, dusty smell that always came on Saturday cleaning day."

Pinpoint: Paragraph 14 reveal moment highlighted with inline before/after rewrite mechanics.
What Generic AI Says

Vague Chatbot Response

"Expand the realization that the vacuum is working, not hunting."

The Breakdown: Expand... how? What sensory cues make this land? AI has no craft framework for theatrical reveals. Just a vague direction.

Result: The writer must invent the technique from scratch.
