STUDIO ETHOS & CONTEXT

Crafting Digital Experience from the Northern Savannah

Independent Frontend Architecture & Design Experiment

01 · The Crossroads of Gombe

Rooted in Gombe, Nigeria, a serene geographic crossroads in the northeastern savannah where ancient trading pathways and regional communities converge. Inspired by the calm, unhurried cadence of northern landscapes and the profound oral history of regional performing arts, this project explores how cultural narrative can be preserved through disciplined digital craft.

02 · Narrative Architecture & Speculative Design

Strata Archive is an independent exploration of digital editorial design and interactive storytelling. Instead of passive browsing, we treat digital platforms as tactile archival exhibitions. Typography, pacing, motion, and white space are orchestrated with the structural rigor of a cinematic shooting script.

03 · Crafting Global Web Standards from Home

Built entirely from an independent workspace in Gombe, this archive demonstrates that award grade web engineering and avant garde visual direction can originate from anywhere. Operating with modern web APIs, 60fps momentum rendering, fluid typography, and glassmorphic micro interactions, the project honors global standards while remaining anchored in local calm.

04 · Honest Creative Positioning

We deliberately avoid fictitious claims of commercial film production or overseas offices. Every component on this page is a transparent study in digital archiving, frontend performance, and visual communication. We welcome dialogue with curators, creative technologists, and designers worldwide.

PROJECT 01 · FEATURE DRAMA · 108 MINUTES

The Sound Cartographer

35MM ANAMORPHIC DRAFT THREE POLISH

Dramatic Premise

“An aging acoustic engineer in an isolated valley isolates an uncatalogued harmonic frequency in historical field tape recordings, uncovering an abandoned settlement erased from official regional cartography.”

Thematic Breakdown and Progression

ACT I · ACOUSTIC ISOLATION

The researcher catalogs natural subterranean resonance patterns until anomalous human voices appear encoded within water flows.

ACT II · UNRECORDED SETTLEMENT

Journeys into unmapped terrain guided solely by binaural audio cues, confronting bureaucratic resistance and personal memory collapse.

ACT III · HARMONIC RESOLUTION

Discovers the physical remnants preserved within limestone acoustics, producing a permanent public sonic archive.

PROJECT 02 · PSYCHOLOGICAL CHAMBER PIECE · 94 MINUTES

Limestone & Silver

CHAMBER ENSEMBLE PACKAGING PHASE

Dramatic Premise

“During an electrical blackout in an archival preservation facility, an archivist and an estranged restorationist race against rising summer heat to salvage rare silent film nitrate negatives before chemical combustion.”

Thematic Breakdown and Progression

ACT I · CHEMICAL CLOCK

Air conditioning fails as temperatures climb toward 38 degrees Celsius, threatening volatile silver nitrate negatives.

ACT II · REPRESSED GUILT

Trapped in the vault corridors, unspoken estrangement erupts while delicate emulsion frames must be manually cooled.

ACT III · SACRIFICE & LIGHT

A choice between preserving the sole existing negative or saving their shared humanity as the first morning light strikes.
