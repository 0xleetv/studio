Totality · The chill

The air changes

The temperature drops by several degrees. Birds fall silent and roost. Around the whole horizon, a 360 degree twilight glows back in.

Light 02 · The last bead

Diamond ring

Sunlight threads the valleys of the moon as the final bead. Francis Baily drew it in 1836.

Nightfall · The umbra

Darkness lands

The shadow's dark core sweeps past at thousands of kilometres per hour, never more than a few hundred kilometres wide. A narrow ribbon where noon turns to night.

Nightfall · Stars at midday

Midnight noon

Venus and the brightest stars step out of a darkened noon sky. Every horizon glows like sunset, because the light is leaving everywhere but here.

Geometry 03 · The shadow

Path centre
