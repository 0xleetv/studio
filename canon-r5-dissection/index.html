THE SPECIFICATION
CORE
SENSOR. 36 by 24 mm full frame stacked CMOS, back illuminated, approx 45 megapixels.
ENGINE. DIGIC X processor with DIGIC Accelerator.
STABILIZATION. Sensor shift, up to 8.5 stops at center, 7.5 at the periphery.
CAPTURE
RECORDING. 8K RAW video up to 60P, captured in camera.
SPEED. Up to 30 frames per second electronic shutter, 12 with mechanical.
SENSITIVITY. ISO 100 to 51200, expandable to 102400.
INTERFACE
VIEWFINDER. 0.5 inch OLED, 5.76 million dots, approx 0.76x magnification.
MONITOR. 3.2 inch vari angle touch screen, approx 2.1 million dots.
FOCUS. Dual Pixel Intelligent AF with Eye Control.
BODY
CONSTRUCTION. Magnesium alloy chassis, water and dust resistant.
STORAGE. One CFexpress Type B and one SD UHS II slot.
WEIGHT. Approx 656 grams, body only.
NOTABLE
Eye Control AF. The camera focuses where you look.
