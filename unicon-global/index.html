Corporate Profile

Dedicated to National Infrastructure Development and Quality Execution

Unicon Global Investment Nigeria Limited is an indigenous limited liability engineering firm and holding company of specialty contractors serving electric utility infrastructure, commercial, and industrial markets throughout Nigeria.

We benchmark all project delivery to international best practices, deploying certified engineers and rigorous quality assurance systems to guarantee safety and hazard free operations in every site community.

Corporate Mission
To optimally utilize local engineering capacities and capabilities to provide effective, qualitative cost, and timely construction work for complete client satisfaction.
Corporate Vision
To build a leading engineering construction company through our readily available indigenous expertise, continually engaging in building and developing our nation Nigeria.

Technical Portfolio

Core Engineering Capabilities

End to end engineering execution from high voltage transmission lines to structural civil development.

⚡

High Voltage Substation Engineering

Complete engineering design, procurement, and construction of transmission substations, distribution step down stations, and switchgear assemblies.

  • Transmission lines and distribution substations
  • Transformer setups up to 33kV and 11kV ratings
  • Power panel coupling and grid interconnection
🌐

Rural and Urban Electrification

Expanding community electricity access through high tension and low tension inter town connection line construction across diverse geopolitical regions.

  • Distribution networks and township power grids
  • Pole line hardware installation and line stringing
  • Community electrification across multiple local government councils
☀

Commercial Solar and Renewable Microgrids

Sustainable captive power installations, industrial photovoltaic arrays, intelligent battery storage banks, and public street lighting systems.

  • Solar street and garden lighting installation
  • Photovoltaic modules, charge controllers, and inverters
  • Direct current and alternating current power conversions
🏗

Civil and Structural Project Works

Heavy infrastructure foundations, commercial facilities, healthcare centers, and industrial building projects supervised by COREN certified engineers.

  • Heavy structural foundations and reinforced concrete works
  • Institutional buildings and corporate facility development
  • HSE compliant site supervision and structural inspections
